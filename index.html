CORE CAPABILITIES

Engineered for Endpoint Forensics & Triage

Defensive modules built to extract, correlate, and establish evidential proof from suspected Windows artifacts.

Endpoint Threat Detection

Performs defensive static PE binary analysis, section entropy scoring, and compiler signature verification to identify ransomware markers and suspicious characteristics.

Digital Forensics

Examines collected investigation artifacts across Windows binaries, script payloads (PowerShell, VBScript, Batch), and network PCAPs to isolate malicious indicators.

Incident Investigation

Organizes triage findings into structured investigation cases, standardizing IOC extraction across IPs, domains, hashes, and persistence registry keys.

Attack Reconstruction

Maps identified behaviors to MITRE ATT&CK techniques and ransomware kill-chain stages, reconstructing adversary progression from initial ingress to impact.

Evidence Traceability

Establishes cryptographic chain of custody using SHA-256 verification and immutable audit records, maintaining integrity for legal and incident reporting.

Investigation Timeline

Chronologically correlates system events, execution timestamps, and forensic observations to provide analysts with a coherent, defensible narrative of the breach.
